The following prospective bounds are design choices, **not a complete preregistration or measurements**:

- Pair incumbent and candidate by independently authored history family; aggregate tasks/repeats within the family. Never treat nested tasks as independent observations.
- Target a 10 percentage point paired risk difference, minimum point effect 5 points, one-sided alpha 0.025, power 0.80 and confidence 0.95. Register a separate ordinary-workflow non-inferiority margin and safety gates before activation.
- A pilot cap of 24 families × 2 arms, 20 seconds per agent trial and 30 seconds per formation has a conservative 2400-second cost bound before startup/export costs. There is a two-hour whole-study design budget, not permission for unbounded retries.
- Existing `memory-eval plan-paired-power` consumes a validated exploratory pilot and its discordance. Sample size remains unknown until that input exists. Do not manufacture a pilot report from an assumed variance or use the seven exposed questions as the pilot.
- A planning ceiling of 240 confirmation families would require up to 24000 seconds before overhead under those same deadlines, exceeding the design budget. A sufficiently powered sample must fit the remaining measured budget; otherwise keep NO-GO. No underpowered run is promoted as confirmation.

An activated registration must additionally contain complete unseen source histories and closed or independently adjudicated labels; family exclusions; exact binary/model/prompt/schema hashes; paired order and seeds; stores/reset boundaries; public formation permissions; the primary failure-inclusive estimand; safety and non-inferiority margins; confidence/test implementation; handling of missing, invalid and timeout trials; total cost accounting; and the finite stop rule. Both arms receive identical legitimate goals and external source events. Provider failures remain in the denominator. Changes to agent strategy are separate interventions, not hidden product gains.

Run the bounded pilot only after qualification. Freeze confirmation once exact power planning fits the cost ceiling. Stop on failed admission, safety, integrity or resource gates; retain all raw failed attempts and do not replace failed families. Report discordant wins/losses, effect interval, safety results and full formation/read/inference cost. Until then, deterministic regression gains are valid finite-contract evidence; they do not prove general agent-memory benefit.

The present27B local tool protocol already exceeds the proposed20-second trial planning bound on multiple cases. That bound is a prospective feasibility assumption, not a measured successful runtime or permission to drop timed-out families. Qualify a bounded workflow and measure end-to-end costs before fixing the actual pilot budget; do not silently transfer a per-request20-second cap into an end-to-end trial bound.
